### About Me

Hi! I am a second-year M.S. student in [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://ece.gatech.edu) at Georgia Institute of Technology. Prior to Georgia Tech, I earned my Bachelor's degree from [School of Vehicle and Mobility](https://eng.svm.tsinghua.edu.cn), Tsinghua University in 2025.

My research interests lie in **AI Safety** and **Mechanistic Interpretability**, with a focus on language and multimodal models in **real-world**, **agentic** settings. I am interested in connecting the evaluation of safety risks with an understanding of the internal mechanisms, and using these insights to develop effective attacks, interventions and defenses. In particular, I aim to make models and agents safer as they interact with computers, external systems, and sensitive information, where safety failures can have consequences beyond the conversation itself.
